### [00:15 - 00:35] The Innovation: The Reality Dial
- **Visual:** Screen capture of the web app. Player starts at the *Power Console*. The player toggles the **Reality Dial** switch from **Cadet** to **Specialist** to **Commander**. The UI smoothly morphs from colorful gauges with icons and Grade 3.6 words to numerical telemetry in kW and mmHg, and finally to governing differential equations.
- **Narration (Voiceover):**  
  *"Meet Sol Keeper. We built a single, deterministic space outpost simulation engine and created the Reality Dial. For younger cadets ages 7 to 12, the station speaks in intuitive status words and icons. For middle schoolers, it speaks in real units. For high schoolers and teachers, it surfaces the underlying physics equations. The simulation underneath never changes—only how it’s explained."*
- **On-Screen Text:**  
  *Cadet (Ages 7–12) · Specialist (Ages 12–14) · Commander (Ages 15+)*

- **Narration (Voiceover):**  
  *"Nothing in Sol Keeper is invented silently. Every constant traces back to authenticated NASA standards: BVAD metabolic rates, OCHMO radiation ceilings, and Ames mass margins. When a solar storm strikes, it’s driven by NASA DONKI space weather telemetry. When crew members conduct research, they cite NASA Open Science Data Repository publications."*

  *59 Authenticated Datasets · BVAD-2022 · OCHMO · NASA DONKI · NASA OSDR · NASA Trek*

### [01:00 - 01:25] Competing Constraints & No Free Choices
- **Visual:** A 60-hour dust storm strikes Jezero Outpost. Solar power drops sharply. The player drags life support above science in the load-shedding priority list. In Life Support, the player activates the Brine Processor Assembly to boost water recovery, and a notification shows the deduction of 1.5 daily crew-hours.
- **Narration (Voiceover):**  
  *"In Sol Keeper, no choice is free. Running the Brine Processor recovers 98% of your water, but costs 1.5 crew-hours every single day. Enriching greenhouse CO₂ speeds up crop growth, but risks lethal hypoxia if untracked. Every incident response forces genuine trade-offs between crew fatigue, scarce spare parts, and permanent hardware efficiency."*
- **On-Screen Text:**  
  *Zero-Sum Trade-offs: Power · Oxygen · Water · Radiation · Crew Time*
