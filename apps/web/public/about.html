Project Overview

Sol Keeper is a browser-based, offline-ready mission simulator designed for students ages 7–12 (with depths flexing to high school and classroom use) that transforms deep space survival constraints into gameplay. Operating lunar and Martian outposts across 5 training missions and 3 historical crisis replays, players make real decisions across five station consoles—balancing life support, power load shedding, radiation sheltering, food production, and communication light delays. Players can jump straight into the action with Fast Play, take on the daily challenge via Mission of the Day, and track live space weather with Today on the Sun. Every number the simulation computes traces directly to authenticated NASA technical standards (BVAD, OCHMO, APR 8070.1) or real space weather and orbital telemetry (DONKI, OSDR, Horizons, Trek). Through the Reality Dial, the same deterministic physics engine is presented at three cognitive depths—Cadet (visual gauges, qualitative status words, Flesch–Kincaid Grade 3.6), Specialist (SI engineering units), and Commander (mathematical rates and equations)—ensuring that no choice is free and every decision teaches something true.

60-Second Judge Path

  1. 0:00 – Launch in One Click:
    Open the application and click Fast Play (Jezero Outpost) (or launch with telemetry via Deep Link) to land directly on the Mission Briefing.
  2. 0:15 – Review Real Space Agency Data:
    Inspect the Martian environmental hazards (30 sols, solar-only power, 60h dust storm). Check Today on the Sun for live/archived NASA DONKI solar flare telemetry and the Astronaut Science card derived from NASA OSDR space biology publications.
  3. 0:30 – Experience the Reality Dial:
    Tap Cockpit to view the command deck. Click Run the Sol to advance mission time. Flip the Reality Dial in the header from Cadet (icons and plain words, Grade 3.6 readability) to Specialist (kW, kg/day, mmHg) to Commander (rate equations). The underlying simulation state remains byte-identical.
  4. 0:45 – Execute a Genuine Trade-Off:
    Open the Life Support Console to activate the Brine Processor Assembly (saving water at the cost of 1.5 daily crew-hours), or open the Power Console to reorder the load-shedding priority list. Every decision enforces a real resource cost—no action is free.
  5. 1:00 – Verify Scientific Traceability:
    Click Data Sources or Data Passport in the navigation chrome to inspect all 59 cataloged datasets, each with primary source links, evidence badges, and DOI/NTRS citations.

The Reality Dial

The central innovation of Sol Keeper is one simulation, presented at three depths:

Reality Dial depth tiers and audience mapping
Depth Audience What changes
Cadet Ages 7–12 (Grade 3.6 reading level) Visual gauges, icons, qualitative status words, guided tutorial.
Specialist Ages 12–14 (Middle school) Real numbers, SI engineering units (kW, kg/day, mmHg, mSv).
Commander Ages 15+ & Classrooms Rate equations, safety thresholds, and underlying physics mathematics.

The simulation itself never changes between levels—only how it is explained. Toggling the dial mid-mission keeps the numbers underneath bit-identical.

Technical Architecture & Verification

  • Deterministic Simulation: Pure TypeScript engine in packages/sim with zero external side effects, validated by 75 test suites and 691 automated tests.
  • Monte Carlo Balance Harness: 4,050 seeds evaluated across idleBot, greedyBot, and prudentBot to guarantee every failure mode is reachable and skilled play is rewarded.
  • Offline-Ready Progressive Web App: Precached via Workbox (121 assets, 10.7 MB total) for spotty classroom connectivity.
  • Privacy & AI Disclosure: Zero player free text collected, stored, or transmitted; zero analytics tracking; zero runtime AI calls. Developed using Claude Code for engineering assistance and verification.
